### Charter of Patient Rights (MoHFW & NHRC Guidelines)
1. **Right to Emergency Medical Care**: Under Supreme Court mandates (Pt. Parmanand Katara vs Union of India) and the Clinical Establishments Act, all hospitals (public and private) are legally bound to provide basic emergency medical care to stabilize a patient, regardless of the patient's ability to pay an upfront deposit. Refusing emergency care for lack of funds is illegal.
2. **Right to Records and Reports**: Patients or their authorized representatives have the right to access their medical records and case papers. Hospitals must provide these within 72 hours of a request.
3. **Release of Dead Bodies**: It is illegal for any hospital to withhold a dead body on the grounds of unpaid medical bills. The Charter of Patient Rights strictly prohibits holding bodies hostage. Hospitals must release the body and pursue legal mechanisms for debt recovery later.

### Grievance Escalation
In cases of medical negligence, refusal of emergency care, or withholding of a body, citizens can immediately escalate by filing complaints to:
1. The Chief Medical Officer (CMO) or District Health Officer.
2. The State Medical Council.
3. The National Human Rights Commission (NHRC).
