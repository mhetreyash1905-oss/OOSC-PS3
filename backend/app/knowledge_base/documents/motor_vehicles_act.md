### E-Challan and Traffic Violations (Motor Vehicles Amendment Act)
1. **Wrongful E-Challans**: If an e-challan is issued wrongfully (e.g., wrong vehicle number captured by camera, fine for helmet when wearing one), the citizen has the right to contest it through the state's Traffic Police Grievance Portal or virtual courts. 
2. **Towing Disputes**: Towing protocols require an announcement before towing. If the vehicle owner arrives before the vehicle is towed, it should be released upon payment of a "no parking" fine without towing charges.

### Third-Party Insurance Claims (Motor Vehicles Amendment Act)
1. **Mandatory Third-Party Insurance**: Under Section 146, no person shall use a motor vehicle in a public place unless there is in force a policy of insurance complying with the requirements of this Chapter.
2. **Hit and Run Compensation**: Section 161 provides compensation for hit-and-run motor accidents. The central government scheme provides fixed compensation for grievous hurt and death resulting from hit-and-run accidents where the identity of the vehicle cannot be ascertained.
3. **No-Fault Liability**: Section 164 provides for payment of compensation in case of death or grievous hurt due to a motor vehicle accident under the principle of no-fault liability, meaning the claimant does not have to plead or establish that the death or grievous hurt was due to any wrongful act, neglect, or default of the vehicle owner.
