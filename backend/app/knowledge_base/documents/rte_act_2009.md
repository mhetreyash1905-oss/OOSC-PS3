### Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act, 2009
1. **Mandatory 25% EWS Quota (Section 12(1)(c))**: All private unaided recognized schools (excluding minority institutions) must reserve at least 25% of their entry-level class seats (Class 1 or pre-school) for children belonging to Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) and disadvantaged groups in the neighborhood. They must provide free and compulsory elementary education to these children.
2. **Prohibition of Capitation Fee (Section 13)**: No school or person shall, while admitting a child, collect any capitation fee (illegal donation) or subject the child or parents to any screening procedure (interviews). Violations attract fines up to 10 times the capitation fee charged.
3. **No Denial of Admission (Section 15)**: A child shall be admitted in a school at the commencement of the academic year or within such extended period as may be prescribed. No child shall be denied admission.

### Grievance Escalation
If a private school refuses an EWS admission under the 25% quota or demands a capitation fee, a formal complaint should be generated and submitted to the District Education Officer (DEO) or the State Commission for Protection of Child Rights (SCPCR) for immediate enforcement of the RTE Act.
