### Section 2(f) — Definition of Information
Information means any material in any form, including records, documents, memos, e-mails, opinions, advices, press releases, circulars, orders, logbooks, contracts, reports, papers, samples, models, data material held in any electronic form and information relating to any private body which can be accessed by a public authority under any other law for the time being in force.

### Section 2(j) — Right to Information
Right to information means the right to information accessible under this Act which is held by or under the control of any public authority and includes the right to: (i) inspection of work, documents, records; (ii) taking notes, extracts or certified copies of documents or records; (iii) taking certified samples of material; (iv) obtaining information in the form of diskettes, floppies, tapes, video cassettes or in any other electronic mode.

### Section 3 — Right to Information
Subject to the provisions of this Act, all citizens shall have the right to information.

### Section 4 — Obligations of Public Authorities (Proactive Disclosure)
Every public authority shall maintain all its records duly catalogued and indexed, publish within 120 days from enactment: particulars of its organisation, functions and duties; powers and duties of its officers; procedure followed in decision making; norms set for discharge of functions; rules, regulations, instructions, manuals and records held; categories of documents available; boards, councils, committees; directory of officers; monthly remuneration of officers; budget allocation; manner of execution of subsidy programmes; particulars of recipients of concessions; details of information available electronically; particulars of facilities available for obtaining information; names, designations and other particulars of PIOs.

### Section 6 — Request for Obtaining Information
(1) A person who desires to obtain any information under this Act shall make a request in writing or through electronic means in English or Hindi or in the official language of the area, to the CPIO or SPIO, specifying the particulars of the information sought, accompanied by the prescribed fee. (2) An applicant making request for information shall not be required to give any reason for requesting the information or any other personal details except those necessary for contacting him. (3) Where an application is made to a public authority requesting for information which is held by another public authority, the PIO shall transfer the application or such part of it to that other public authority within 5 days.

### Section 7 — Disposal of Request
(1) Subject to proviso, the CPIO/SPIO shall as expeditiously as possible and within 30 days of receipt of the request, either provide the information on payment of prescribed fee or reject the request for reasons specified in sections 8 and 9. Proviso: where information concerns the life or liberty of a person, it shall be provided within 48 hours. (2) If the CPIO/SPIO fails to give decision within the period specified, the request shall be deemed refused. (3) Where a decision is made to provide information on payment of further fee, the CPIO/SPIO shall send intimation with details of fees, the amount of fee determined and requesting the applicant to deposit the fee. (5) No fee shall be charged from persons below poverty line. (6) Where the public authority fails to comply with time limits, information shall be provided free of charge.

### Section 8 — Exemptions from Disclosure
(1) There shall be no obligation to give any citizen information that would prejudicially affect: (a) sovereignty and integrity of India, security, strategic, scientific or economic interests, relations with foreign state; (b) has been expressly forbidden by court or tribunal; (c) would cause breach of privilege of Parliament or State Legislature; (d) commercial confidence, trade secrets, intellectual property; (e) information available in fiduciary relationship; (f) information received in confidence from foreign government; (g) endanger life or physical safety of any person; (h) impede process of investigation or apprehension or prosecution; (i) cabinet papers including records of deliberations of Council of Ministers, Secretaries; (j) personal information with no relationship to public activity or interest, which would cause unwarranted invasion of privacy. (2) Notwithstanding anything in the Official Secrets Act 1923, a public authority may allow access if the public interest in disclosure outweighs the harm to the protected interests.

### Section 19 — Appeal
(1) Any person who does not receive a decision within the time specified, or is aggrieved by a decision, may within 30 days prefer an appeal to officer senior in rank to CPIO (First Appellate Authority). FAA shall dispose of appeal within 30 days, extendable to 45 days with recorded reasons. (3) A second appeal against the FAA decision shall lie within 90 days to the Central Information Commission or State Information Commission.

### Section 20 — Penalties
(1) Where the CIC/SIC is of the opinion that the CPIO has without reasonable cause refused to receive application, not furnished information within time, malafidely denied request, knowingly given incorrect/misleading/incomplete information, destroyed information, or obstructed furnishing of information, it shall impose penalty of Rs. 250 per day until application is received or information furnished, total penalty not exceeding Rs. 25,000. (2) The CIC/SIC shall also recommend disciplinary action against the CPIO under service rules.

### Section 22 — Act to Have Overriding Effect
The provisions of this Act shall have effect notwithstanding anything inconsistent therewith contained in the Official Secrets Act, 1923, and any other law for the time being in force or in any instrument having effect by virtue of any law other than this Act.

### Filing Procedure and Fees
Application fee: Rs. 10 for Central Government departments (payable via Indian Postal Order, Demand Draft, Banker's Cheque, or cash). State governments may prescribe different fees. Additional fees for providing information: Rs. 2 per page (A4/A3), actual cost for larger pages, Rs. 50 per diskette/floppy, inspection of records is free for first hour, Rs. 5 for each subsequent 15 minutes. BPL applicants are exempt from all fees. Mode of filing: Written application (on plain paper, no prescribed form) or electronic submission where available.
